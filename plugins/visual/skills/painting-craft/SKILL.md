# Painting craft

Parts fail at 2× zoom while the composition passes. Use whatever the project draws with; this is
the method, not the tool. Read first: taste (`references/taste.md`); `references/recipes.md` for
foliage, grass, metal, cloth, windows, lights or dark passages; `references/technique.md` before
drawing. Brief research (step 2) to a subagent on a cheaper model. Skip only inapplicable steps,
and say so.

1. **Tradition:** two or three named works, plus one public-domain reference image per hero
   element (museum open-access collections, Wikimedia Commons), saved beside the art. An era names
   a subject, not a style (`references/aesthetic.md`).
2. **Research** canon, history and real sizes, with sources; mark guesses UNVERIFIED
   (`references/armour.md`).
3. **Space and light:** horizon, camera, px per metre per depth plane (`references/scale.md`);
   one light, its direction, colour and fill.
4. **Masses** and a value plan; render and look at it. A weak thumbnail fails at any detail.
5. **Hero studies** alone at 2×, from their recipe and `references/anatomy.md`. Put each beside
   its reference at the same size and list the structural gaps; redraw until none remain.
6. **Compose** the studies at their depth planes; the rest from recipes, less detailed.
7. **Ground:** contact, shadows, depth order.
8. **Unify:** one light, one medium pass, rendered at 2×.
9. **Review zoomed in:** `references/review.md`.

Commit message: proxies, UNVERIFIED items, what was reviewed and how.
