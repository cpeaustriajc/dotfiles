# Armour and costume: failures seen

Use museum and period sources for any kit; research per `SKILL.md` step 2. The knight's kit: `worked-example.md`.

**Layering and fit.** Radii grow outward wherever layers overlap: cloth under plate smaller, or the
plate vanishes; plate 1-2 outside the padding; straps 0.4 outside their surface.

**Checks**
- Helmet and pauldron sizes against museum sizes (Met Chalcis, makers' spec pages).
- Visor type, slits and breaths from the references, before drawing a helmet.
- Dates: a Milanese harness is about 1450. Flag a game set that disagrees with history, then follow
  the user.
- Doublet, pauldrons and colours from the subject's source art (cover or concept art), not a generic museum harness.
- Mail at a ring size the medium keeps, draped like heavy cloth.

**Other periods.** Brief a research subagent for shapes and sizes, not essays: each piece's period
term, shape and size with a source URL; layering order; where the source art departs from history;
UNVERIFIED on anything unsourced. References are the spec; when they disagree, follow the majority
and name the one set aside. When canon and history disagree, say so, then do what the user asked.
