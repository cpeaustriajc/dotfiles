# Armour and costume: failures seen

Use museum and period sources for any kit; research per `SKILL.md` step 2.

**Henry's kit** (Bohemia, 1403, after the KCD2 cover art): globose breastplate over a red quilted
arming doublet with long dagged shoulder strips; dagged red skirt to mid-thigh with mail showing
through the slits; black belt with a long strap end, brown hip belt; bascinet with a bow-fronted
visor and aventail; hourglass gauntlets; legs read darker than the polished torso. Sword
(Radzig's): blade 89, total 120, taller than a seated man's head.

**Layering and fit.** Radii grow outward wherever layers overlap: cloth under plate smaller, or the
plate vanishes; plate 1-2 outside the padding; straps 0.4 outside their surface.

**Mistakes made**
- Helmet and pauldron oversized until checked against museum sizes (Met Chalcis, makers' spec
  pages).
- A houndskull drawn where the references showed a bow-fronted visor: read visor type, slits and
  breaths.
- Dates mixed: Milanese harness is about 1450; KCD2's endgame set is the "Noble" set. Flag it, then
  follow the user.
- A generic museum harness ignored the cover's red doublet and missing pauldrons.
- Mail smeared into a slab in the oil pass: a ring size the medium keeps, draped like heavy cloth.

**Other periods.** Brief a `researcher` agent for shapes and sizes, not essays: each piece's period
term, shape and size with a source URL; layering order; where the game's art departs from history;
UNVERIFIED on anything unsourced. References are the spec; when they disagree, follow the majority
and name the one set aside. When canon and history disagree, say so, then do what the user asked.
Canon kits built against: Ghost of Tsushima (Kamakura, 1274), Sekiro, Onimusha: Way of the Sword,
Night City and Los Santos (clothing and cars from stills).
