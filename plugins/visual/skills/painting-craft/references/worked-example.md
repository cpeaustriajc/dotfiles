# Worked example: measurements from past pictures

Read with `scripts/knight.py`. Sizes in centimetres. They belong to those pictures; for another
subject, measure it.

- **Henry's kit** (Bohemia, 1403, after the KCD2 cover art): globose breastplate over a red quilted
  arming doublet with long dagged shoulder strips; dagged red skirt to mid-thigh with mail showing
  through the slits; black belt with a long strap end, brown hip belt; bascinet with a bow-fronted
  visor and aventail; hourglass gauntlets; legs read darker than the polished torso. Sword
  (Radzig's): blade 89, total 120, taller than a seated man's head.
- **Seated Henry** (against a trunk): hip joint 9.5 above the seat, shoulder joint 58-60, crown
  92-95; raised thigh at 45° puts the knee centre 31 above the hip.
- **Pebbles**, a 150 cm mare: body length ≈ withers height (longer reads as a dachshund), head 0.4
  of the height.
- **Mutt**, a medium dog, 52 cm withers: body 1.1 × withers. A face under 25 px doesn't read: use
  silhouette and coat pattern.
- **Cars:** Los Santos coupe (2013): 450 long, 130 tall, wheelbase 245, wheels 66. Night City wedge
  (2077): 470 long, 115 tall, wheelbase 270, wheels 72.
