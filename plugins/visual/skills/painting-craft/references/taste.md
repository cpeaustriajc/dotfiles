# Taste

Two people can ask for the same picture and reject each other's result. Taste decides the
tradition, the finish and how hard the review will be.

1. Read the project's taste note, if it keeps one (for example `.claude/rules/painting-taste.md`,
   or a note its `CLAUDE.md` points to). Then skip the questions.
2. Otherwise ask briefly: which artists or works they love for this kind of picture; whether they
   zoom in on detail or view from a distance; sketch or finished piece; what disappointed them
   before. Offer two or three named traditions rather than an open question.
3. Offer to save the answers as that note, in their words, each followed by the rule it implies.
   Update it when a review teaches something new.

A sketch for a distant viewer still needs a sound space and value plan (steps 3-4 of SKILL.md),
not measured hands.
