# Sentence rules (ASD-STE100 and Global English)

- Name the real symbol, file, flag or command. Define a named pattern at first use.
- Write an instruction as a command of at most 20 words, with its condition or warning first.
  Keep any other sentence to 25 words and a paragraph to 6 sentences.
- Keep "the", "a" and "that" where they show the structure.
- Keep "only" and "not" next to the word they change. Make each "it", "they" and "this"
  point at one thing.
- Break up noun strings: "the script that checks the proto-import budget".
- Call each thing by one name everywhere.
- Use periods, not semicolons, em dashes or slashes. Use no idioms or Latin abbreviations.
- Leave out "simply", "easy" and "quickly" in a procedure. Link text says where it goes.

When a rule makes a sentence worse, fix the sentence another way. A sentence that obeys every
rule and sounds machine-written has failed.
