# The product interview

Read this before round 1. The steps are in `SKILL.md`. What changes from a grill:

- **Breadth first.** Round 1 asks one question per branch before going deep on any: who it is
  for, their problem today, whether it is worth it, the destination, how we'll know it worked
  (grill's done at this level), the first version's cut, and the riskiest unknown.
- **The ledger grows the product.** Each settled branch becomes a line; show the Features list
  growing each round, numbered F1…Fn.
- **Vague may stay vague.** When no lookup or prototype would settle it, put it under Not yet
  specified and move on; pressing a vague answer into a feature invents one.
- **Make it concrete** through one person in one moment ("Ana's domain lapses on Friday night.
  What does she do first?"), or offer a rough sketch, mock headline or one-screen flow.
- **The riskiest unknown gets a test, not an opinion**: make it the first item of the first
  project.
