# From personal to sellable

Read this when something I built for myself might sell. The steps are in `SKILL.md`.

Run the worth-it check again, for others:

- **I am user one, which is evidence and bias at once.** Ask for evidence from others: who has
  asked for it or a copy, who I could show it to this week, how I would reach more of them.
- **Price what selling costs** in weeks before the verdict: multi-user accounts, billing,
  onboarding without me, support, privacy and security, reliability.

The usual verdict is test first: five people with the problem, or an offer before building the
selling parts. On build, write a new Draft product for others with product phases; the personal
one stays until the new one replaces it.
