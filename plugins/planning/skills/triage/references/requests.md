# Requests with no item, and direct orders

Read this when I ask what needs triage without naming an item, or give a direct order such as
"move #42 to ready-for-agent". `SKILL.md` says when.

## What needs attention

List three groups, oldest first, one line each with `[issue]` or `[PR]`:

1. No state label.
2. `needs-triage`.
3. `needs-info` with a reporter reply after the last triage note.

PRs: only from authors outside the team, unless I name one. Show the counts. Write nothing. I
pick the next item.

## Direct orders

"Move #42 to ready-for-agent": say in one line which labels change and what you post, then do
it, skipping the checks. With no brief yet, ask once if I want one.
