# Methods

Read this before you pick a method. The steps are in `SKILL.md`. Each method fits an afternoon.

- **My own itch.** List the manual tasks I repeated three or more times in the last month and the
  paid tools that annoyed me. A task I keep rebuilding for myself is the strongest signal there is.
- **Follow the money.** Where do people already pay for this outcome, before any AI: invoices,
  freelancers, a clunky incumbent, a published revenue figure in a directory of verified startup
  revenue. Small proven earners are better than big empty markets. Then ask what niche, locale
  or angle they leave open.
- **Complaints about incumbents.** In one narrow category, read 50 two- and three-star reviews
  of the paying tools. Tag each by theme and count; keep the themes that repeat across two or more
  competitors.
- **A community's own words.** Read a week of a community's top threads I belong to or can join.
  List problems in their words, and work they pay someone to do by hand.
- **Search demand.** A purchase people already search for, with a modifier that repeats (a city,
  an industry, a use) so one product can answer many searches.
- **An ecosystem's gaps.** The "missing feature" threads and low reviews in a marketplace with
  built-in discovery: a store's apps, a platform's plugins. A small paid add-on is a good first
  step before a full product.
- **Unbundle.** One feature of a big tool that a niche uses alone, done better for them.
