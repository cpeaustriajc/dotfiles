# Methods

Read this before you pick a method. The steps are in `SKILL.md`. Each fits an afternoon.

- **My own itch.** Manual tasks I repeated three or more times last month, and paid tools that
  annoyed me.
- **Follow the money.** Where people already pay for the outcome (invoices, freelancers, a
  clunky incumbent, a directory of verified startup revenue); small proven earners beat big empty
  markets. Ask what niche, locale or angle they leave open.
- **Complaints about incumbents.** 50 two- and three-star reviews in one narrow category; keep
  themes that repeat across two or more competitors.
- **A community's own words.** A week of top threads in a community I belong to or can join.
- **Search demand.** Programmatic SEO: a purchase people search for, with a repeating modifier.
- **An ecosystem's gaps.** "Missing feature" threads in a marketplace with built-in discovery;
  a small paid add-on is a good first step.
- **Unbundle.** One feature of a big tool that a niche uses alone.
