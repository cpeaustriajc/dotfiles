# Launch video

Failures, cheap to prevent early and costly late: flat motion (one fade curve), a voice that
gives itself away, off-message copy, broken frames, and a video with nowhere to go (no covers,
captions or post copy; files left in scratch).

Skip a step only when it does not apply, and say so. Scripts: `scripts/` (`--help`).

1. Rules, brief, capture and storyboard per `references/brief.md`. Get the storyboard approved.
2. Build the master per `references/hyperframes.md`. Lint, snapshot, look at each.
3. Voice per `references/voice.md`; retime the film to it.
4. Music per `references/music.md`; models per `references/models.md`.
5. Render and review per `references/review.md`; open files per `references/quicktime.md`.
6. Adapt to 1:1 and 16:9 per `references/hyperframes.md`.
7. Launch kit per `references/launch-kit.md`.
8. Clean up models and scratch only after approval; say what went.
9. Before each handover, check every shape against `references/review.md`.
