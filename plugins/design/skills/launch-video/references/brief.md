# Rules, brief, capture, storyboard

Step 1 of `SKILL.md`, in this order.

1. **Rules.** List the project's rules that bind copy: brand (colours, type, mark, voice),
   positioning (what the product is never framed as), legal or billing limits (no advice, no
   unpaid features), and people who must not appear (an anonymous founder). Look in the wiki,
   `CLAUDE.md` and memory notes. Check every script line, on-screen line and post against the
   list. For a missing rule, ask with an example: "Can it say 'no bank login'? If bank
   connections may come later, it would have to be pulled."
2. **Brief.** One question each, each with an example of what it changes: the angle (what the
   first two seconds show), sound (voice, music, or silent for muted autoplay), the master shape
   (usually 9:16 for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, then 1:1 and 16:9), and where the files must end
   up (a wiki, a drive, the repo), because scratch folders are deleted.
3. **Capture** the live site's brand tokens, fonts, real copy and example figures. It is the best
   source of copy and keeps the video from inventing claims. Rebuild its screens as live HTML
   components in its own style, not screenshots.
4. **Storyboard** about six frames as a table (frame, beat, on screen, why): value stated by the
   second frame, the name saved for the payoff. Get approval before building: a change costs a
   minute here and a render after the build.
